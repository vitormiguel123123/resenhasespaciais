INTERSTELLAR TOURISM



Enzmann Starship, concept for crewed interstellar travel done by Dr. Robert Enzmann.

Interstellar voyages involve spacecraft traveling between stars in the universe, potentially aiding in the discovery of planets capable of harboring life. These journeys will also help us learn more about new stars, planets, and solar systems, and could eventually enable humanity to colonize a new solar system.
Achieving this requires specific technology; one of the most crucial factors to consider is the vast distance and the time such journeys would take. Advances in propulsion technology could resolve the issue of time during these trips. In the future, robots and probes will likely be deployed before human beings are put at risk; thus, these voyages will vastly expand our horizons, allowing us to understand the universe far better than ever before.
