INTERSTELLAR TOURISM

Theories, Prototypes and Limitations.



What is our topic?

Our site will focus on space tourism (naturally), which is the concept of taking ordinary people to various destinations in space for leisure, business, or other purposes. This type of tourism can be categorized in various ways, ranging from flights that remain within the atmosphere to those that reach outer space beyond Earth's atmosphere. These are the two currently feasible types, with companies like SpaceX, Virgin Galactic, and Blue Origin offering rather expensive trips while taking a crucial step toward the expansion of space exploration. This evolution could lead to lunar, orbital, and ground tourism, local system tourism, and—hopefully—interstellar tourism, which would explore star systems beyond our own, such as TRAPPIST-1, a system with seven confirmed planets and Proxima Centauri (Alpha Centauri C) the nearest system to ours , with two confirmed planets, among many others. This section of the site will cover the beginnings of space exploration, the methods used to reach these systems, and the ways to reach them while remaining alive.
